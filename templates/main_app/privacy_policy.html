Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026 (previous version: April 13, 2019)

The Ofek Foundation ("us", "we", or "our") operates the https://www.theofekfoundation.org website (the "Service"). This page explains what personal data the Service collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Most of the Service (the games and tools) works without an account and without sending us any personal data.

Information We Collect

Account Information (only if you create an account)

Accounts are optional. If you register or log in, we receive:

  • Your email address
  • Your username (the display name you choose)
  • If you log in with Google or GitHub: the basic profile information that service shares with us (your name, email address and profile picture link) and an account identifier

Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see your password; Firebase stores it in hashed form.

Game Settings

When you save a game's settings, they are stored in your browser. If you are logged in, they are also stored with your account, so they follow you to other devices.

Hangman Challenge Mode

Guesses in the final round of Hangman are checked by our server. To limit how often anyone can guess, the server briefly keeps your IP address in memory (for a few seconds). It is not stored.

Usage Data

We use Google Analytics to understand how the Service is used: for example, which pages are visited, the type of browser and device, approximate location, and the time and date of visits. Google processes your IP address to provide this.

Cookies and Browser Storage

  • Analytics cookies. Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart.
  • Browser storage. The Service stores your saved game settings, your sign-in session (kept by Firebase Authentication) and your username in your browser's local storage, so the site remembers them. Logging out clears your session and the settings saved in that browser.

You can block cookies or clear your browser's storage at any time. The games and tools still work; you will just be logged out and your saved settings in that browser will be cleared.

Use of Data

We use the data above only to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service, including accounts and saving your settings
  • Send you emails you ask for, such as password reset emails
  • Understand how the Service is used so we can improve it
  • Keep the Service secure and prevent abuse

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service Providers

The Service runs on Google infrastructure. These providers process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Hosting and Cloud Functions) stores account information and game settings, and serves the website. Data is stored in the United States. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Analytics. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google and GitHub, only if you choose to log in with them.

For more information on Google's privacy practices, see the Google Privacy & Terms page. For GitHub's, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep account information and saved settings until you ask us to delete them. To delete your account and its data, email us at the address below.

If you had an account on the previous version of this website, your old account (username, email address and saved game settings) has been kept so it can be restored on the new site. Email us if you would like it deleted instead.

Transfer Of Data

Your information is processed and stored in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those where you live. By using the Service, you agree to this transfer.

Disclosure Of Data

The Ofek Foundation may disclose your Personal Data in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation
  • Protect and defend the rights or property of The Ofek Foundation
  • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Service
  • Protect the personal safety of users of the Service or the public
  • Protect against legal liability

Security Of Data

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Links To Other Sites

Our Service may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us. If you click on a third party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies or practices of any third party sites or services.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 18 ("Children").

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your Children has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.

Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new Privacy Policy on this page and update the "effective date" at the top.
